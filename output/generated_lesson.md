# Introduction to RAG  

*(RAG = Retrieval‑Augmented Generation)*  

---

## What  

**Retrieval‑Augmented Generation** means that a computer first **searches** for useful pieces of text, then **gives those pieces to the AI**, and finally the AI **writes an answer** using that extra information.  

- **Retrieval** – find relevant text from a collection (books, articles, web pages, etc.).  
- **Augmented** – the found text is added to the AI’s knowledge for this one answer.  
- **Generation** – the AI creates a response based on its own training **and** the retrieved text.  

---

## Why  

Sometimes the information we need is  

* **New** – it was created after the AI was trained.  
* **Private** – it exists only inside a company or a school and is not on the public internet.  

The AI alone cannot know these facts because it only remembers what it learned during training. By **retrieving** the latest or private text, the AI can answer correctly even for brand‑new or hidden topics.

---

## How  

1. **Search** – The system looks for short passages that might answer the question.  
2. **Pass to AI** – Those passages are attached to the prompt, so the AI can read them.  
3. **Write** – The AI combines its own knowledge with the supplied passages and writes the final answer.  

The AI’s own model does **not** change; it only uses the retrieved text for this single reply.

---

## Example  

### Question  

> *What is the main reason for the 2023 Indian monsoon delay?*  

### Retrieved source text  

> “According to the Indian Meteorological Department’s report released in July 2023, the monsoon delay was mainly caused by an unusually warm sea surface temperature in the Indian Ocean, which slowed the rise of moist air.”  

### Answer generated by the AI  

> The 2023 Indian monsoon was delayed mainly because the sea surface in the Indian Ocean was unusually warm. This warmth reduced the upward flow of moist air, which is needed for the monsoon to start on time.  

*The answer uses the information from the source text and the AI’s general knowledge about weather.*

---

## Limits  

- **Wrong or old information** can be retrieved. If the searched text is outdated or mistaken, the AI may repeat that error.  
- Retrieval **does not retrain** the AI. The model’s core knowledge stays the same; only the extra text influences the current answer.  
- The system depends on the quality of the searchable collection. If the needed document is missing, the AI will answer only from its original training.  

---

## Recap  

- **RAG = Retrieval‑Augmented Generation** – search, give to AI, write.  
- It helps when we need **new** or **private** facts that the AI did not learn during training.  
- The process has three clear steps: **retrieve**, **provide as context**, **generate**.  
- The method can still give **incorrect or outdated** answers and it never changes the AI’s underlying model.  

Use RAG whenever you have a reliable source of up‑to‑date text and you want the AI to include that exact information in its reply.
